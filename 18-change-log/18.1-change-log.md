## Version 2.0 – 2026 SOP Review

Version 2.0 represents a comprehensive review of the SLSSA Standard Operating Procedures, incorporating member feedback, operational review, identified gaps and improvements to the structure and navigation of the SOP set.

### New Procedures

- Added [SOP 1.7 – Member Injury Reporting](https://sops.surflifesavingsa.com.au/1-work-health-and-safety/1.7-member-injury-reporting.html), creating a dedicated process for reporting and recording injuries to SLS personnel.

- Added [SOP 3.12 – Rescue Ready](https://sops.surflifesavingsa.com.au/3-lifesaving-standards/3.12-rescue-ready.html), defining minimum personnel, equipment, IRB and communications readiness requirements.

- Added [SOP 3.13 – Gear and Equipment Disposal](https://sops.surflifesavingsa.com.au/3-lifesaving-standards/3.13-gear-and-equipment-disposal.html) to provide requirements for the disposal of lifesaving gear and equipment.

- Added [SOP 12.1 – Command, Control and Coordination Operations](https://sops.surflifesavingsa.com.au/12-emergency-management-and-response-operations/12.1-command-control-and-coordination-operations.html), establishing the command, control and coordination framework for emergency management operations.

- Added [SOP 14.4 – Problem UAVs (Drones)](https://sops.surflifesavingsa.com.au/14-vessels-and-aircraft/14.4-problem-uavs-drones.html) to provide reporting, separation and escalation requirements for unauthorised or problem UAVs affecting lifesaving operations.

### Work Health and Safety, Licences and Equipment

- Updated [SOP 1.2 – Drugs and Alcohol](https://sops.surflifesavingsa.com.au/1-work-health-and-safety/1.2-drugs-and-alcohol.html) to clarify that the prohibition applies while a person is on duty.

- Updated [SOP 4.1 – Licences](https://sops.surflifesavingsa.com.au/4-rescue-vessels/4.1-licences.html) to clarify licence requirements for rescue vessels and vehicles and that government licences do not replace required Surf Life Saving qualifications or endorsements.

- Updated [SOP 4.6 – Marine Mammals](https://sops.surflifesavingsa.com.au/4-rescue-vessels/4.6-marine-mammals.html) to introduce minimum PPE requirements when handling or working near marine mammals, including gloves, face mask, protective eyewear and long sleeved clothing.

- Updated [SOP 5.1 – Lifesaving Vehicles](https://sops.surflifesavingsa.com.au/5-gear-and-equipment/5.1-lifesaving-vehicles.html) to correct references to lifesaving vessels and expand vehicle authorisation, compliance, equipment, branding and amber warning light requirements.

- Updated [SOP 5.2 – Side By Side Vehicles (SSVs)](https://sops.surflifesavingsa.com.au/5-gear-and-equipment/5.2-side-by-side-vehicles-ssvs.html) to clarify P plate requirements for provisional licence holders and the ability for clubs and services to apply local driver authorisation requirements.

- Updated [SOP 5.6 – Oxygen Resuscitation Equipment](https://sops.surflifesavingsa.com.au/5-gear-and-equipment/5.6-oxygen-resuscitation-equipment.html) to correct clinical terminology from “oropharyngeal masks” to “oropharyngeal airways”.

- Updated [SOP 5.7 – Automated External Defibrillators (AED)](https://sops.surflifesavingsa.com.au/5-gear-and-equipment/5.7-automated-external-defibrillators-aed.html) to clarify that AED kits should be stored in a water resistant carry case, with a waterproof pelican type case preferred.

- Updated [SOP 5.8 – Methoxyflurane](https://sops.surflifesavingsa.com.au/5-gear-and-equipment/5.8-methoxyflurane.html) to clarify that methoxyflurane is commonly known as Penthrox or the “green whistle”.

### Communications

- Updated [SOP 7.1 – Radio Communications](https://sops.surflifesavingsa.com.au/7-communications/7.1-radio-communications.html) to require the designated Patrol Captain radio to remain available for the full patrol and for SOC to be notified if it is unavailable or unserviceable.

- Updated [SOP 7.2 – Radio Equipment Specifications](https://sops.surflifesavingsa.com.au/7-communications/7.2-radio-equipment-specifications.html) to provide a pathway for extended radio approvals for external agencies and stakeholders with an ongoing operational requirement.

- Expanded [SOP 7.4 – Communications Security](https://sops.surflifesavingsa.com.au/7-communications/7.4-communications-security.html) to clarify requirements for sensitive operational information, recording and releasing communications, and social media.

- Updated [SOP 7.7 – Call Signs](https://sops.surflifesavingsa.com.au/7-communications/7.7-call-signs.html) to reflect current operational call signs, including RWC assets designated RWC 1–10 and radio call signs JET SKI 1–10.

### Patrol Operations

- Restructured [SOP 8.1 – Beach Management Principles](https://sops.surflifesavingsa.com.au/8-patrol-operations-general/8.1-beach-management-principles.html) and introduced separate procedures for [SOP 8.1.1 – Patrol Captain Responsibilities](https://sops.surflifesavingsa.com.au/8-patrol-operations-general/8.1.1-patrol-captain-responsibilities.html), [SOP 8.1.2 – Senior Lifeguard Responsibilities](https://sops.surflifesavingsa.com.au/8-patrol-operations-general/8.1.2-senior-lifeguard-responsibilities.html) and [SOP 8.1.3 – Patrol Team Responsibilities](https://sops.surflifesavingsa.com.au/8-patrol-operations-general/8.1.3-patrol-team-responsibilities.html).

- Updated [SOP 8.4 – Conclusion of Patrol Operations](https://sops.surflifesavingsa.com.au/8-patrol-operations-general/8.4-conclusion-of-patrol-operations.html) to include Rescue Ready arrangements for IRBs at the conclusion of patrol operations.

- Expanded [SOP 8.6 – Beach Vehicle Operations](https://sops.surflifesavingsa.com.au/8-patrol-operations-general/8.6-beach-vehicle-operations.html) with clearer requirements for operational beach driving, beach access, terrain and environmental considerations.

- Expanded and renamed [SOP 8.9 – Marine Pollution and Environmental Contamination](https://sops.surflifesavingsa.com.au/8-patrol-operations-general/8.9-marine-pollution-and-environmental-contamination.html) to address harmful algal blooms, fish kills, foam, odours and airborne irritants and provide clearer controls for patrol modification and member safety.

- Updated [SOP 8.10 – Beach Attendance Monitoring](https://sops.surflifesavingsa.com.au/8-patrol-operations-general/8.10-beach-attendance-monitoring.html) to align beach attendance reporting to the start, middle and end of patrol using consistent water, beach and watercraft categories.

- Expanded [SOP 8.11 – Adverse Weather and Environmental Conditions (“Inclement Weather”)](https://sops.surflifesavingsa.com.au/8-patrol-operations-general/8.11-adverse-weather-and-environmental-conditions.html) to include severe weather, heat, harmful algal blooms, airborne hazards, patrol modification, withdrawal and Duty Officer endorsement.

### Emergency Operations

- Completed [SOP 9.2 – Lost or Missing Persons](https://sops.surflifesavingsa.com.au/9-patrol-operations-emergency/9.2-lost-or-missing-persons.html), replacing the previous “To be issued” placeholder with an operational response procedure.

- Updated [SOP 9.3 – Requesting an Ambulance](https://sops.surflifesavingsa.com.au/9-patrol-operations-emergency/9.3-requesting-an-ambulance.html) to clarify the Position, Problem, People, Progress reporting format and redefine “Progress” as what is occurring to progress the incident.

- Updated [SOP 9.4 – Requesting Helicopter Support](https://sops.surflifesavingsa.com.au/9-patrol-operations-emergency/9.4-requesting-helicopter-support.html) to clarify the rostered Duty Officer’s authority to approve helicopter tasking where deployment may provide operational benefit.

- Updated [SOP 9.10 – Tsunami Operations](https://sops.surflifesavingsa.com.au/9-patrol-operations-emergency/9.10-tsunami-operations.html) to identify the Joint Australian Tsunami Warning Centre as the authoritative warning source and recognise potential SOC relocation or degraded capability during a land warning.

- Updated [SOP 10.1 – Reduced Visibility Operations](https://sops.surflifesavingsa.com.au/10-reduced-visibility-operations/10.1-reduced-visibility-operations.html) to define civil twilight and reduced visibility and clarify authorised operational response capability in low light and night conditions.

### Search and Rescue

Completed [Section 11 – Search and Rescue Operations](https://sops.surflifesavingsa.com.au/11-search-and-rescue-sar-operations/11.1-sar-responsibilities.html), replacing the previous “To be issued” procedures with operational guidance covering:

- SAR responsibilities
- SAR stages
- Risk versus gain
- SAR information factors
- SMEAC briefings
- Parallel line search patterns
- Creeping line search patterns
- Expanding square search patterns
- Underwater search and rescue
- Information exchange during transfer of command
- Conclusion of SAR operations

### Emergency Management and Response Operations

Renamed and expanded Section 12 as **Emergency Management and Response Operations**.

Changes include:

- Added [SOP 12.1 – Command, Control and Coordination Operations](https://sops.surflifesavingsa.com.au/12-emergency-management-and-response-operations/12.1-command-control-and-coordination-operations.html).

- Renumbered and expanded [SOP 12.2 – State Operations Centre (SOC) – SURFCOM](https://sops.surflifesavingsa.com.au/12-emergency-management-and-response-operations/12.2-state-operations-centre-soc-surfcom.html).

- Renumbered and clarified [SOP 12.3 – Surf Emergency Response System (SERS) (“13 SURF”)](https://sops.surflifesavingsa.com.au/12-emergency-management-and-response-operations/12.3-surf-emergency-response-system-sers.html).

- Updated [SOP 12.4 – Surf Club Emergency Response Teams (ERT)](https://sops.surflifesavingsa.com.au/12-emergency-management-and-response-operations/12.4-surf-club-emergency-response-teams-ert.html) within the expanded section.

- Renumbered [SOP 12.5 – Coastal Safety Camera Network (CSCN)](https://sops.surflifesavingsa.com.au/12-emergency-management-and-response-operations/12.5-coastal-safety-camera-network-cscn.html).

- Moved [SOP 12.6 – Operations App](https://sops.surflifesavingsa.com.au/12-emergency-management-and-response-operations/12.6-operations-app.html) into Section 12, separating system guidance from operational reporting requirements.

### Operational Reporting

Created **Section 13 – Operational Reporting** to separate reporting requirements from the systems used to record them.

- Added [SOP 13.1 – Patrol Reporting](https://sops.surflifesavingsa.com.au/13-operational-reporting/13.1-patrol-reporting.html), consolidating patrol reporting requirements including start, middle and end of patrol reporting.

- Renamed and restructured [SOP 13.2 – Incident Reporting and Recording](https://sops.surflifesavingsa.com.au/13-operational-reporting/13.2-incident-reporting.html), separating incident reporting requirements from the Operations App used to record them.

### Vessels and Aircraft

- Updated [SOP 14.1 – Helicopter Landing Site](https://sops.surflifesavingsa.com.au/14-vessels-and-aircraft/14.1-helicopter-landing-site.html) to resolve conflicting landing site dimensions, confirming 30 m × 30 m as the minimum and 50 m × 50 m as recommended.

- Updated [SOP 14.2 – Over Water Helicopter Interaction](https://sops.surflifesavingsa.com.au/14-vessels-and-aircraft/14.2-over-water-helicopter-interaction.html) so the procedure applies across powered rescue watercraft, including IRBs, RWCs and JRBs, rather than being primarily IRB focused.

- Expanded [SOP 14.3 – RPAS Operations (Drones)](https://sops.surflifesavingsa.com.au/14-vessels-and-aircraft/14.3-rpas-operations-drones.html) to include organisation wide authorisation, governance, privacy, tasking and reporting requirements.

- Added [SOP 14.4 – Problem UAVs (Drones)](https://sops.surflifesavingsa.com.au/14-vessels-and-aircraft/14.4-problem-uavs-drones.html) to provide reporting, separation and escalation requirements for unauthorised or problem UAVs affecting lifesaving operations.

### Critical Incident Management and Member Support

Renamed and expanded Section 15 as **Critical Incident Management and Member Support**.

- Renamed and expanded [SOP 15.1 – Critical Incidents](https://sops.surflifesavingsa.com.au/15-critical-incident-management-and-member-support/15.1-critical-incidents.html).

- Renamed and broadened [SOP 15.2 – Operational Debriefing](https://sops.surflifesavingsa.com.au/15-critical-incident-management-and-member-support/15.2-operational-debriefing.html) beyond critical incidents and corrected links to member welfare support.

- Expanded [SOP 15.3 – Member Welfare and Critical Incident Support](https://sops.surflifesavingsa.com.au/15-critical-incident-management-and-member-support/15.3-member-welfare-and-critical-incident-support.html) to provide clearer member support pathways.

### Site Wide Improvements

- Standardised terminology, naming, operational call signs and cross references throughout the SOP set, including use of “SLS personnel”, gender neutral terminology and “Surfguard”.

- Corrected broken internal links and outdated section references.

- Corrected malformed tables, heading inconsistencies, image and display issues and other navigation and formatting defects.

- Consolidated duplicated requirements and improved cross references between related procedures.
